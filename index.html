🏗️ Fence Type & Dimensions

Fence Type Selection

  • Chain Link: Most economical, ideal for security & commercial use
  • Galvanized: Steel finish, max durability, no coating
  • Black Vinyl Coated: Decorative chain link, residential favorite
  • Wood Privacy: Dog-ear or board-on-board, residential privacy
  • PVC/Vinyl: Low maintenance, premium residential
  • Ornamental/Wrought Iron: Decorative, high-end residential & commercial
📏 Post Specifications

Post Sizing Guide

  • Line posts: 1-5/8" standard, 2" heavy duty for commercial
  • Terminal posts: Always minimum 2-3/8" for chain link
  • Gauge: 9 gauge standard residential, 6 gauge commercial
  • Embedment: 1/3 of total post height minimum
🚪 Gate Requirements

Gate Tips

  • Measure gate opening in inches for shop drawings
  • Walk gates: 36"–48" typical. Drive gates: 10'–20' or more
  • Double gates need a drop rod / cane bolt at center
  • Cantilever gates best for uneven ground
🔱 Barbed Wire & Special Options

Barbed Wire & Add-Ons

  • Barbed wire requires arm extensions on posts
  • 3-strand adds ~8–10% to post material cost
  • Razor wire (concertina) is commercial/industrial only
  • Privacy slats add visual screening to chain link

🏛️ Permits & Regulatory Compliance
Permit requirements, setback compliance, and application forms

📚 Tab 6 Tutorial — Permits

  • Always verify permit requirements with the local municipality before starting work
  • Permit fees vary widely — $50 to $500+ depending on location and fence value
  • Most municipalities require a site plan showing fence location and setbacks
  • Processing time: 1–6 weeks typical. Rush processing available in some jurisdictions
📍 Municipality & Jurisdiction

Municipality Selection

  • Select the municipality where the fence will be installed
  • Requirements vary by zone within the municipality
  • Corner lots often have stricter setback requirements
📏 Height & Setback Compliance

Setback Rules (Typical)

  • Front yard: Max 1.0m (3.3 ft) solid, 1.5m (5 ft) open in most municipalities
  • Side/Rear yard: Max 1.8m (6 ft) typical residential
  • Corner lots: Sight triangle requirements apply — check locally
  • Setback from property line: 0" to 6" typical (confirm with municipality)

⚠️ Utility Locates Sheets
Underground utility identification — Call Before You Dig

📚 Tab 7 Tutorial — Utility Locates

  • MANDATORY in Ontario (and most provinces): Call Ontario One Call (1-800-400-2255) or 811 at least 5 business days before digging
  • Utility companies mark their lines with colored flags/paint
  • You MUST avoid marked zones — work within safe clearance distances
  • Keep a copy of locates ticket on site for inspection
  • Ticket valid 30 days in Ontario — refresh for long projects
☎️ ONTARIO ONE CALL: 1-800-400-2255 or online at www.ontarioonecall.ca — Call at least 5 business days before excavating. FINE UP TO $50,000 FOR NON-COMPLIANCE.
