## Workflow notes
- Tabs 1-7 collect the operational inputs required for pricing.
- Tab 8 calculates the estimate automatically from specs, layout, labor, and catalog pricing.
- Tab 9 locks approved pricing before contract issue.
- Tabs 10-14 manage execution, documentation, and closeout.
- Tabs 15-17 support administration, pricing maintenance, and geographic planning.
